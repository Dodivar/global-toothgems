# Global Toothgems — Project Context

## Product

Global Toothgems is a premium but approachable e-commerce and online-training platform focused on dental/tooth gems and related professional education.

The product should feel:
- professional;
- friendly and reassuring;
- premium;
- modern;
- playful without looking childish;
- visually polished and trustworthy.

## Inspirations

Use these references for UX, positioning or visual inspiration only:
- https://www.amybeautystudio.com
- https://www.dentluxe.com.au/fr/products/free-pdf-catalogue-18k-gold-tooth-gems
- https://sparklebymaya.com.au/collections/workshops
- https://www.trebbih.com

Do not copy branding, layouts, text, assets, distinctive components or proprietary content.

Explicitly avoid reproducing the identity or design of:
- https://toothgemsworld.com

The references are mood/benchmark sources, not implementation specifications.

## Brand direction

Primary visual base:
- black;
- white;
- pastel blue: RGB(185, 205, 229).

CTA/accent direction:
- vivid pale emerald green and/or fuchsia pink.

Typography:
- Montserrat as the principal UI/content typeface.
- A script/parfumerie-style font may be used sparingly for decorative initials/lettrines only.

Visual language:
- refined glassmorphism inspired by modern Apple/iOS interfaces;
- significantly less blur than exaggerated glassmorphism;
- generous whitespace;
- strong typography hierarchy;
- elegant product presentation;
- subtle motion;
- accessible contrast.

## Languages

The storefront is intended to support:
- French;
- English;
- German.

Do not hard-code customer-facing text in application logic when localization is expected.

## General product principle

Prefer simple, reliable flows over feature quantity.
Every feature must have a clear user value.
Avoid premature abstractions and speculative architecture.
