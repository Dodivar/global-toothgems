# Global Toothgems — Project Context

## Product

Global Toothgems is a premium but approachable e-commerce and online-training platform focused on dental/tooth gems and related professional education.

The product should feel:
- professional;
- friendly and reassuring;
- premium;
- modern;
- playful without looking childish;
- visually polished and trustworthy.

## Inspirations

Use these references for UX, positioning or visual inspiration only:
- https://www.amybeautystudio.com
- https://www.dentluxe.com.au/fr/products/free-pdf-catalogue-18k-gold-tooth-gems
- https://sparklebymaya.com.au/collections/workshops

Do not copy branding, layouts, text, assets, distinctive components or proprietary content.

Explicitly avoid reproducing the identity or design of:
- https://toothgemsworld.com

The references are mood/benchmark sources, not implementation specifications.

## Brand direction

Primary visual base:
- black;
- white;
- pastel blue: RGB(185, 205, 229).

CTA/accent direction:
- vivid pale emerald green and/or fuchsia pink.

Typography:
- Montserrat as the principal UI/content typeface.
- No script/parfumerie-style font on the website: it was removed for legibility. Accents stay in Montserrat.

Visual language:
- refined glassmorphism inspired by modern Apple/iOS interfaces;
- significantly less blur than exaggerated glassmorphism;
- generous whitespace;
- strong typography hierarchy;
- elegant product presentation;
- subtle motion;
- accessible contrast.

## Audience and markets

- Tooth-gem artists and beauty professionals (tools, stones, kits, training);
- consumers discovering tooth gems, often from Instagram, on a phone;
- customers in France and the EU first; prices in EUR.

## Languages

- Launch: French (reference language) and English.
- Planned: German (enabled in the database, not launch-blocking), other EU languages later.

Customer-facing text is always localized (see `08-content-and-localization.md`).

## Product surfaces

- Storefront (shop, product pages, gift cards, Loyalty Club page).
- Academy (training catalogue, sales pages, lesson player).
- Member area (`/compte`: orders, courses, certificates, reviews, favourites, loyalty, profile, security).
- Studio 3D (`/studio-3d`): design tool for tooth-jewellery compositions, planned as a paid subscription.
- Back office (`/admin`) for the team.

## General product principle

The product is being industrialised for a real commercial launch: reliability of the existing
surfaces comes before new features.
Prefer simple, reliable flows over feature quantity.
Every feature must have a clear user value.
Avoid premature abstractions and speculative architecture.
