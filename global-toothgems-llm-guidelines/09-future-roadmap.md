# Future Roadmap

These items came from the original project notes but are intentionally separated from MVP.

## Phase 2 — Commerce growth

### Loyalty
Example direction:
- 5 qualifying purchases above €50;
- unlock a 20% benefit.

Requires precise business rules before implementation.

### Referral
- referral links/codes;
- attribution;
- rewards;
- anti-abuse rules.

### Advanced gifting
- richer gift-card/voucher workflows;
- scheduling;
- recipient messaging if desired.

## Phase 2 — Learning depth

### Advanced quizzes
- interactive identification tasks;
- “which tooth is this?” interactions;
- richer question types;
- branching assessments;
- step-by-step prerequisites.

### Certification
- final score;
- passing threshold;
- diploma generation;
- polished certificate design;
- downloadable certificate;
- shareable certificate;
- social sharing.

### Graduation experience
- confetti;
- completion celebration;
- graduate navigation;
- graduate-only resources.

## Phase 3 — Community

### Forum
- course discussions;
- learner profiles;
- moderation;
- notifications;
- search.

### Discord-style community
Potentially integrate with Discord rather than rebuilding every community capability internally.

The note “Discord type stras dentaire” should be interpreted as a community experience benchmark, not as a requirement to clone Discord.

## Phase 3 — Gamification

Potential model:
- XP;
- levels;
- badges;
- achievements;
- learning streaks;
- progression rewards.

Do not introduce an XP economy without first defining how it affects learner motivation and product behavior.

## Roadmap implementation rule

Future features may influence extensibility decisions, but must not:
- add unnecessary MVP UI;
- introduce speculative database complexity;
- slow down checkout;
- complicate authentication;
- weaken security;
- make MVP acceptance criteria ambiguous.
